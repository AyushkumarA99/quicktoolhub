functions Calendar Arithmetic & Leap Years

Calculating age is more intricate than dividing total days by 365, because Gregorian calendar months vary in length (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years occur every 4 years.

1. If Target_Day < Birth_Day, borrow days from the previous month (considering Feb 28/29, 30, or 31).

2. If Target_Month < Birth_Month, borrow 12 months from Target_Year.

3. Exact Years = Target_Year - Birth_Year.

help Frequently Asked Questions

What if I was born on February 29th?

Leap day birthdays are handled seamlessly. In non-leap years, your birthday milestone is recognized on February 28 or March 1 depending on calendar conventions.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Yes! Set the "Calculate Age As Of" input to any future date (for retirement planning, insurance forms, or driver's licenses) to see how old you will be.
