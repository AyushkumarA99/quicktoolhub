functions GST Calculation Formulas

GST Exclusive (Adding GST to Base Amount):
GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) ÷ 100
Total Price = Base Amount + GST Amount
GST Inclusive (Extracting GST from Gross Amount):
Base Amount = (Gross Amount × 100) ÷ (100 + GST Rate)
GST Amount = Gross Amount - Base Amount

help Frequently Asked Questions

What are CGST and SGST?

Under intra-state GST, the tax is divided equally between the Central Government (CGST) and the State Government (SGST). For inter-state transactions, the combined tax is applied as Integrated GST (IGST).
