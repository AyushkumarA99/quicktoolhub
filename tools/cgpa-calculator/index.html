checklist How to Calculate Your CGPA

  1. 1
    Select Scale: Pick the 10-point scale or 4-point scale matching your university guidelines.
  2. 2
    Enter Grades and Credits: For each subject, choose your letter grade (or enter direct points) and the course credit weighting (e.g. 3 or 4 credits).
  3. 3
    Instant Weighted Result: The calculator multiplies each course grade by its credits, sums the totals, and divides by your total credits.

functions The Credit-Weighted Formula

CGPA = Sum of (Course Grade Point × Course Credits) ÷ Sum of (Course Credits)

Percentage = CGPA × Conversion Factor (e.g. 9.5 or 10.0)

* Note: Conversion formulas vary by university. The AICTE / CBSE standard formula uses 9.5, but some colleges use 10.0 or proprietary conversion tables. Check with your university registrar.

help Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA is typically your grade point average for a single academic semester or term, whereas CGPA (Cumulative GPA) reflects your overall academic performance across all completed semesters.

What if a course has 0 credits?

Non-credit or pass/fail audit courses do not factor into weighted CGPA calculations. Only credit-bearing courses are counted.
