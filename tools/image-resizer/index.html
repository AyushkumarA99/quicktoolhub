help Frequently Asked Questions

Does resizing reduce picture quality?

Downscaling preserves crispness via bicubic browser resampling. Upscaling beyond original dimensions naturally has diminishing returns as native pixels are stretched.

Are my pictures private?

100% private. Your images never leave your computer or touch an external server.
