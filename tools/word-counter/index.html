calculate How Counts Are Calculated

Words: Words are separated by white spaces, tabs, or new lines. Repeated consecutive spaces are collapsed so they never inflate your count.

Characters: We provide two counts:

  • With Spaces: The raw Unicode string length, standard for Twitter/X posts (280 characters) and meta descriptions.
  • Without Spaces: Only pure alphanumeric and punctuation symbols, commonly used by academic publishers and translators.

Reading Time: Estimated based on standard silent reading velocity of 200 words per minute (WPM) for adult readers:

Reading Time (minutes) = Total Words / 200

help Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored or uploaded to any server?

No. All word calculations, character counting, and transformations occur 100% locally in your browser's JavaScript memory. None of your text is sent over the network.

Does it support languages other than English?

Yes! Word Counter uses Unicode whitespace and character patterns, making it compatible with Spanish, French, German, Hindi, and most global alphabets.

What is the maximum text size allowed?

Because processing is handled directly by your browser, you can safely paste long essays, book chapters, and research papers up to hundreds of thousands of words without lag.
