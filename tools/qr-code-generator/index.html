verified Reed-Solomon Error Correction Explained

QR codes use mathematical Reed-Solomon error correction algorithms to ensure data can still be recovered even if the barcode is smudged, torn, or partially obscured:

Level L (Low): ~7% Best for digital screens with clean displays and minimal complexity.
Level M (Medium): ~15% Default standard for commercial product labels and flyers.
Level Q (Quartile): ~25% Recommended when QR codes are placed outdoors on billboards.
Level H (High): ~30% Ideal for print banners or when embedding center logos.

Do these QR codes ever expire?

No. These are static QR codes where your data is encoded directly into the pixel pattern. They work permanently without subscription fees or redirects.
