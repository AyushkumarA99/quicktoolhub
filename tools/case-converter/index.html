help Frequently Asked Questions

Does Title Case handle small words?

Title Case capitalizes all primary words while respecting punctuation. For slug generation, symbols and spaces are safely converted into clean hyphens.

Are multiple line breaks preserved?

Yes, line breaks and paragraphs are preserved across prose modes (uppercase, lowercase, title, and sentence case).
