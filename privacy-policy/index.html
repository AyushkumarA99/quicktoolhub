Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Learn how QuickToolHub handles information, browser-based tools, contact messages, and third-party services.

At QuickToolHub, accessible from https://ayushkumara99.github.io/quicktoolhub/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information that is collected and recorded by QuickToolHub and how we use it.

1. How Our Tools Process Data

The vast majority of utilities on QuickToolHub (including Word Counter, Case Converter, Age Calculator, Percentage Calculator, QR Code Generator, Image Resizer, Image Compressor, CGPA Calculator, GST Calculator, and JSON Formatter) operate entirely inside your client web browser.

For tools that run entirely in your browser, such as text converters, calculators, and image utilities, the relevant input is generally processed locally by your browser. We do not intentionally upload the content you enter into these client-side tools to our own server. This statement does not cover information you choose to submit through the Contact Us form or information handled by third-party services.

2. Log Files

Like almost all websites, QuickToolHub follows a standard procedure of utilizing log files for hosting diagnostics. These files log visitors when they visit web pages. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement around the website, and gathering demographic information.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers, request deletion of collected personal data, and request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data not sell the consumer's personal data.

We do not intentionally sell personal information submitted to QuickToolHub. Privacy rights and response deadlines depend on the laws that apply to the request. Please contact us to make a privacy-related request.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
